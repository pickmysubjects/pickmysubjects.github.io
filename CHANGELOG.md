# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

The first full version. The Beta label and the "full version coming" notice are gone.

- Plan board: the whole degree on one board. Mark subjects done with their marks; every degree rule is checked, with the details in one place.
- Share a plan with a short link (imports as a new plan); compare plans on a page of their own.
- New tools: a revision planner (a research-based day-by-day timetable that names what and how to revise, with calendar export and print), a final-exam mark calculator and a WAM calculator.
- Fuller "What students say" for the most-reviewed subjects, in four parts: difficulty and content, assessments, how to study, what to watch out for.
- Key dates on the home page, an exam-time view, and census, exam and results dates in your calendar (.ics).
- A new look across every page, a menu for every feature, site-wide search, and majors with their prerequisite route.

## 0.9.1 — 2026-10-27

A hotfix. The site is labelled Beta until 1.0.0.

- Add to my plan: any semester of your plan can be picked, past ones too. Semesters the subject doesn't run in show a warning instead of being left out.
- Beta label on the header, the version pill and the footer.
- Home page notice: the full version with the 2027 Handbook is coming by end of November.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-27

A redesign. Version 1.0.0 follows in November with the 2027 Handbook.

- New Liquid Glass look and a new logo.
- Plan board: prerequisite lines, clearer markers, bundled links, and a subject sheet that opens from any subject.
- Filters in a sidebar on desktop and a sheet on phones.
- Record page summary and a "My degree" card.
- One-row navigation with a section stepper.
- Better readability and contrast across all 9 languages.
- Fixes for iOS, Android and Windows.
- Analytics now count visits per site language.
